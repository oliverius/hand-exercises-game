Seeing the hand points means the hand is tracked; its posture may still be uncertain. The camera preview shows the predicted gesture and hand side. Lower thresholds accept lower-confidence gestures and may make more mistakes. Changing this setting stops the current practice.

Use one hand. With the camera ready, hold one finger pointing up for ¾ second to start a 3-second countdown, or press Start for 10 seconds. Open your palm to begin a hold; close your hand to finish. Stop at any time. Manual mode works without making a particular gesture.

The classifier can miss a partially open hand. These timings are practice records, not clinical measurements. Personal calibration is a future enhancement. Camera processing stays on your device; no pictures or recordings are saved.
